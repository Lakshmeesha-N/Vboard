THE PROBLEM

Typing in your own language is a relay race

Today, sending one message in your language means leaving the app, converting it elsewhere, and pasting it back — then fixing what broke.

    Recreated scenario
    8 steps · ⏱ ~45 sec

    That is the cost every single time — for a message, an email, a form, a comment. The current system taxes every sentence you write in your own language.

    THE APP

    Why use Vboard

    One keyboard that replaces the whole relay. It works wherever your keyboard appears — messages, mail, forms, comments.

    🎙

    Speak

    Talk in Kannada, Hindi, English, Telugu — or mix them. Speech becomes clean text in your own language, right in the box.

    ✏️

    Edit

    Messy in, clean out — rewrite the whole text box by voice. Undo is always one tap away.

    ✨

    Ask

    Type @agent and the keyboard writes or answers inside the text box — drafts, replies, explanations.

    No switching apps. No copy-paste relay. Speak, fix, send.

    HOW IT LOOKS

    A keyboard, not another app

    The voice controls live on the keyboard itself. Real screenshots from the app.

    HOW IT WORKS

    Two buttons, one backend

    Voice and questions travel two short paths through the same server, and both land back in your text box.

    🎙 Mic→/v1/process→Speech to text→Insert / rewrite
    ✨ Ring→/v1/agent→Router (starts with @agent?)→Cleanup or Agent + search
    ⌨️ The keyboard applies the result — with Undo
    PRIVACY

    Your words stay yours

    No audio storedVoice is processed and dropped — never saved.
    No text loggedThe server counts requests, never reads content.
    Open sourceAnyone can inspect what the code actually does.
